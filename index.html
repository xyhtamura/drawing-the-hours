Drawing the Hours

A Meadow Gesture Study — Visit I: Morning Verge
Draw cord with mouse or touch. Hold or cross to separate pockets.
Keyboard: [Space] Pause • [M] Sound • [H] Field Book • [R] Restart

Meadow Field Book

Harmonious relationships observed during quiet morning visits. Draw temporary cotton cord around moving creatures to reveal their connections.
Single Loop

Kindred Pair

Enclose exactly two butterflies of the same species (2 Brimstones, 2 Azures, or 2 Roseates). They synchronize wingbeats and leave in peace.

Single Loop

Trio of Flight

Enclose three butterflies representing all three distinct species: 1 Brimstone, 1 Azure, and 1 Roseate.

Single Loop

Seed & Wing

Enclose one butterfly alongside an airborne dandelion seed drifting on the morning breeze.

Figure-Eight

Cross-Meadow Correspondence

Cross the cord to form two distinct pockets: place a kindred pair in Pocket A, and another kindred pair in Pocket B.

Figure-Eight

Nursery Polarity

Place winged travelers in one pocket, and wild drifting dandelion seeds in the opposite pocket.

Hazard

Ground Crawler (Snail)

Snails crawl slowly along the meadow verges. If enclosed in a flight gesture, their heavy shell snarls and frays the cord.
