**Image 1**

How to catch butterflies:

Draw a loop around 2 or more butterflies of the same color or 3 or more butterflies that are different colors. The more butterflies in a loop, the more points you will get.

Sometimes, a butterfly will have a colored spot. Draw a loop around it to change its color.

Catch the number you need before the sun sets or your game is over.

Click your mouse to pause and unpause the game.

There are three kinds of butterflies:

Goliaths are big and slow

Skippers are runty and quick

and Monarchs are right in the middle.

Every fifth level, a special one appears. Catch it to see what happens.
