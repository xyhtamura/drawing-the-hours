Drawing the Hours

Bullets:

1. Dawn 1
    1. Another morning of standing, waiting for the bus.    
    2. I like to watch the insects here, in this patch by the curbside.
    3. Maybe I have an overactive imagination.
	4. In my thirties, I still don't feel like an adult sometimes.
	5. It's hard to save, and prices keep getting higher.
	6. This is not how I imagined adulthood.
2. Noon 1
    1. Today, for lunch, I bought myself a sandwich.
    2. I don't know how much longer my job will exist.
    3. This atrium makes me feel calmer.
	4. At my age, my mother already held a high company position.
	5. I was already six years old.
	6. It's hard for me to think of a future like that.
3. Dusk 1
    1. I always find myself walking home through here.
    2. I can hear the cicadas. And sometimes I can't. 
    3. Bringing home medicine today.
	4. Some health coverages are almost nominal.
	5. The long way home, where I pass by tall grasses and scrap metal
	6. Some places absorb what developments expel.
4. Midnight 1
    1. I'm trying to sleep.
    2. I dream about endless rows of car lights. 
    3. I don't know if I like the day shift or night shift more.
	4. I wake up often, and there's always rustling in the corner.
	5. Just tracing figures, while the world moves around me. 
	6. And the world does move. Because tomorrow keeps happening.
