Drawing the Hours

Bullets:

1. Dawn 1
    1. Another morning of standing, waiting for the bus.    
    2. I like to watch the insects here, in this patch by the curbside.
    3. Maybe I have an overactive imagination.
2. Noon 1
    1. Today, for lunch, I bought myself a sandwich.
    2. I don't know how much longer my job will exist.
    3. This atrium makes me feel calmer.
3. Dusk 1
    1. I always find myself walking home through here.
    2. I can hear the cicadas. And sometimes I can't. 
    3. Bringing home medicine today.
4. Midnight 1
    1. I wake up often, and there's always rustling in the corner.
    2. I trace figures, while the world moves around me.
    3. And the world does move. Because tomorrow keeps happening.
