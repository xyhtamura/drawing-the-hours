# Fish references

The eight levels draw on wild Philippine freshwater fish. These are
visual and movement inspirations; the field is an imagined place, not a
single river assemblage. Recipes, school sizes, resting intervals, and speeds
are designed for play. Story text remains for Xyh.

| Game name | Taxon | Reference | Game treatment |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ricefish | *Oryzias luzonensis* | Luzon freshwater species; [Catalog of Fishes](https://researcharchive.calacademy.org/research/ichthyology/catalog/fishcatget.asp?spid=53309) | Small, pale bodies and gentle drifting movement. |
| Blue goby | *Stiphodon atropurpureus* | Recorded in the Philippines and elsewhere; [Maeda and Palla, 2015](https://mapress.com/zt/article/download/zootaxa.4018.3.3/37431/38815), pp. 392–393 | Dark body, blue stripe, rests on stones followed by darts between them. |
| Spotted barb | *Barbodes montanoi* | Mindanao distribution, golden sides, dark spots, and warm fin colours; [Tan and Husana, 2021](https://lkcnhm.nus.edu.sg/wp-content/uploads/sites/10/2021/09/RBZ-2021-0054.pdf), pp. 317–318 | Simplified spots and three-fish schools that gather and spread. |
| Halfbeak | *Dermogenys palawanensis* | Palawan freshwater species; [Catalog of Fishes](https://researcharchive.calacademy.org/research/ichthyology/catalog/fishcatget.asp?spid=66420) | Slender body, projecting lower jaw, steady glides interrupted by turns. |

The blue goby is native rather than Philippine-endemic. Leyte is the locality
of its neotype, not the original type locality; see the
[catalogue entry](https://researcharchive.calacademy.org/research/ichthyology/catalog/fishcatget.asp?genus=Stiphodon&tbl=species).
The barb reference supports Mindanao; the game does not extend that range to
the Visayas. The halfbeak's Palawan endemism is also discussed in
[Cervancia et al., 2007](https://lkcnhm.nus.edu.sg/app/uploads/2017/04/55rbz141-145.pdf), p. 145.

The three linked papers are held in `library/drawing-the-hours-aquarium/`
with Markdown caches. Their first-page titles and lengths were checked:
Tan and Husana, 15 pages; Maeda and Palla, 15 pages; Cervancia et al., 5 pages.
The attempted download of Parenti's 2008 ricefish monograph returned HTTP 403;
the ricefish identity and locality above come from the catalogue instead.
No captive-care or breeding claims are used in the game.
